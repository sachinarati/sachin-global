What “impact” means on this map

Project delivery means a website was coordinated and published. Audience reach means the YouTube analytics screenshot showed views from that country. Home, work, study and roots are personal connections. These are different kinds of evidence and should not be treated as equal.

For countries without a recorded item, the card says “No connection recorded yet” — that does not prove there has been no impact.
